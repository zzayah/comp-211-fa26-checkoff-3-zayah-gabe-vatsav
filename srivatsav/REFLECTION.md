### Reflection on AI conversation

Gemini Conversation: https://share.gemini.google/hG6lHYXiBGaB

I asked Gemini to explain XOR encryption in the way a student taking COMP 211 would be able to follow. I asked the chatbot about any misunderstandings and other questions that I had. Later, I wrote a simple C program and checked it with the bot to see if my implementation was correct.

Some prompts that I asked the chatbot:

1. I am a student taking COMP 211, which is a systems programming course at UNC chapel hill. Explain XOR encryption at my level
2. how can XOR encrypt and decrypt using the same key? could you explain that in detail
3. got it, but i dont see how using simple XOR encryption is risky, can you show me a flaw?

Apart from these, I asked other follow up questions to the responses the LLM gave me. At the end, I checked my implementation and learnt some design patterns in C from the review I got of the code, which will definitely help me in the future. I was able to understand the difference between One Time Pad (OTP) and Vernom Cipher; I thought the Cipher was a direct implementation of OTP. I also learnt the meaning behind the "end to end encryption" that Whatsapp uses to protect its users privacy.

After the semester ends, I will invest more time in learning more about ciphers and other algorithms related to how ciphers work, and research about better ways to encrypt messages.
